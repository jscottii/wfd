# WFD Meals · Changelog

Versions are MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH:
- **PATCH** (1.1.x) — small tweaks and fixes.
- **MINOR** (1.x.0) — new features.
- **MAJOR** (x.0.0) — big milestones (e.g. the app-store launch, a redesign).

## 1.22.0 — Nov 28, 2026
- **Plan's cost bar now matches Shop:** once your trip is planned, the bar shows the trip's own grocery total. While you're still changing meals it estimates the same way the trip does (it was counting some items twice). If you set a weekly budget, it shows how far over or under you are.
- **The WFD button:** bigger, centered letters, and at most three options with larger, easier-to-read labels, each one about the page you're on (Plan: pick meals, add a recipe, plan ahead · Shop: add to list, switch list, scan a receipt · Pantry: add, scan, use what expires soon · Recipes: add from a link, find new, type one in).
- **Greeting:** a bigger "Good morning, Jon 👋" that shows once, on the first page you open.
- **Welcome questions:** "What matters most" adds *I have dietary restrictions* (asks which diets or allergies) and *Picky eaters at home* (Pick for me skips spicy or unusual dishes). A new question combines how you like to cook (quick meals, a few nights a week, or one big prep day) with an optional weekly budget. Your name is now the last question.

## 1.21.0 — Nov 28, 2026
- **The WFD button:** a round WFD button now sits in the middle of the bottom bar on every page. Tap it for what you can add from there: a recipe, something for the shopping list, something for the pantry, a meal pick, a receipt. It replaces the separate + and cart buttons.
- **Running cost on Plan:** as you pick meals, a bar shows roughly what the shopping list will cost, from the prices you've paid and typical prices.
- **Quick look at any recipe:** tap a recipe's photo anywhere (Recipes, Find new, Prep) for the same preview as the Plan page.
- **Shop:** where you start comes first, then the trip, the map and the list. The title shows the week, like "Nov 30 week's list".
- **Recipes:** filters fit on one line.
- **Your name:** add your first name when you sign up (or any time in Settings), and each page greets you.
- **Welcome questions:** new accounts and guests get five quick, skippable questions (your name, how much detail, leftovers, what matters most, who you cook for) that set things up for you.
- **Repeat meals:** a new setting for how often one meal can show up in a week: never, twice (leftovers once) or up to three times.
- A gentle nudge to name your household if it's still called "Our household".

## 1.20.0 — Nov 27, 2026
- **WFD Meals:** the app's official name, with a new logo ("meals" under the WFD letters) on the app icon, home screen, website and Chrome extension.
- **Plan:** swipe a meal left to empty just that spot, or right to take it out of the week's plan, with Undo. Tap a meal for a quick preview: ingredients, steps, the flow chart and nutrition.
- **Shop:** starting from "Here" or a typed address now finds stores near that place, not near home, and the route ends where you started.
- **Delivery:** no store sign-ins anymore. Copy your list, open the grocery app you already use (Instacart, Walmart, Amazon Fresh, Target, Safeway…) and paste it in.
- **Your own items on the list:** add anything you want to buy, even if it's not for a meal. It stays on the list until you buy it.
- **Prep:** open sections are shaded so you can tell what's open, and a note at the top says which view you're in, with a switch to the other.
- **Settings:** "Who you cook for" is now "How much detail": Simplified, or Give me all the details. There's now just one Prices section.
- **Pantry:** your pantry comes first, with places folded up to keep it tidy. One line shows how many things expire soon, with Update and "Use in this week's plan". Add items with the + button. No cart button on this page.
- **Recipes:** filter by ingredients you type in and by cuisine, sort (newest, A–Z, favorites, calories, protein), and tap ♡ to favorite.
- Chrome extension 1.2: new name and icon.

## 1.19.0 — Nov 26, 2026

## 1.11.0 — Nov 24, 2026
