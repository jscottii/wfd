WFD Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Use Contact us at the bottom of More in the app, or the form below, or email support@wfdmeals.app. Add your email if you'd like a reply.

Common questions

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password?. We email you a 6-digit code; enter it with your new password.

How do I share the app with my household? Go to More → Settings → Household and invite their email. They create an account with that email and join your household automatically.

Timers don't ring. Allow notifications for WFD in your phone's Settings. In the app, keep "Keep screen on" switched on during cook day.

Prices look wrong. Tap a price on the Prices screen to set it yourself, or check a receipt; receipt prices are used first.

How do I delete my account? More → Settings → Delete my account. See our privacy policy.
