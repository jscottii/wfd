About WFD

WFD — What's for Dinner is a small, independent app for families and anyone who cooks for a household. It started from a simple frustration: deciding what to make every night, then making three trips to the store to make it.

WFD puts the whole week in one place. You plan the meals once; WFD builds one shopping list, prices it at the stores near you and finds the cheapest trip, then turns the week's recipes into a single cook-day plan. It keeps track of your pantry, so you buy only what you need.

What it costs

WFD is free to use, with ads. WFD Plus removes the ads. You can try everything without an account; guest data is kept for 14 days.

The recipes and guides

The recipes and guides on this site are written for WFD. Nutrition is estimated from the ingredients and is a guide, not medical advice.
