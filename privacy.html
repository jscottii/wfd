Privacy Policy

WFD Meals · Last updated November 28, 2026

WFD Meals helps a household plan weekly meals, shop for them and cook them. This page explains what the app stores, why, and how to delete it.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our database provider, Supabase).
  • Guests (no account): if you choose Continue without an account, we create a private, temporary sign-in with no email or name attached. What you do as a guest is stored the same way as an account's data, but only that browser can reach it. Guest data is deleted automatically 14 days after the guest started, or when you leave guest mode. If you create an account before then, everything moves to your account and isn't deleted.
  • Your household's data: recipes, weekly plans, shopping items, prices you enter or that the app looks up, pantry items, purchase history and the results of receipts you check. Receipt photos are read on your device and are not uploaded or stored. When you scan barcodes to fill your pantry, only the barcode number is looked up (with Open Food Facts). If you use "photo of a shelf or fridge", that photo is sent once to Anthropic's AI to list the foods it sees; it isn't stored by WFD, and Anthropic doesn't use it to train its models.
  • Settings: your home address (used to plan shopping routes), store choices and preferences.
  • Meal preferences: diets, allergies and foods to avoid, used only to suggest and flag recipes for your household.
  • Messages you send us: what you write in Contact us, the app version, and the email you give if you want a reply.
  • Usage information: which features are used and when — for example "opened the app", "started a plan", "saved a recipe", "tapped the Instacart button", "an ad was shown or tapped" — with your account, household and the app version. When you plan a shopping trip, we also note whether it started from home, your current location or a typed address, the general area it started in (rounded to about 10 km, never an exact spot) and which store chains were on it. We use it to understand what's working, fix problems, and count ad views. It's kept in our own database (no outside analytics companies) for up to 2 years, and deleted with your account.
  • Your location, only when you ask: if you tap "Use my current location" or start a trip from "Here", your location is used to fill in an address or plan that trip. We keep the address you save, not your movements.

Ads

The free version of WFD may show ads, including on planning, shopping and cooking screens. Ads are provided by Google AdSense on the web and Google AdMob in the phone apps. Google and its partners may use cookies or your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads, and — only if you agree — to personalize them. On iPhone you'll be asked for permission before any tracking; you can say no and still use the app. You can manage Google ad personalization at adssettings.google.com; see how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. WFD Plus members never see ads.

How your data is stored and shared

  • Everything is stored with Supabase in the United States, encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Your household's data is only visible to the people in your household. The app's owner can see totals (like how many people use a feature) and the messages you send us.
  • We don't sell your data. We share it only with the services listed below, and only what each one needs to do its job.
  • Payments for WFD Plus are handled by Stripe, Apple or Google; we never see your card number.

Services the app uses

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, server functions), hosted in the United States.
  • OpenStreetMap, Nominatim and OSRM for maps, addresses and drive times. They receive the addresses and store locations being looked up, not your account.
  • SerpApi to find products when you send a list to Walmart. It receives the item names, not your account.
  • Instacart, Walmart and Amazon receive your shopping list when you tap their button, and you sign in with them directly. (Amazon links include our Associates tag, so we may earn a small commission on qualifying purchases.)
  • Open Food Facts for product names when you scan a barcode, and Anthropic (Claude) to read shelf or fridge photos when you use that option.
  • TheMealDB for recipe ideas and photos, and recipe websites when you save a recipe from a link.
  • Google AdSense / AdMob for ads in the free version, and Stripe for WFD Plus payments on the web.
  • The Bare Minimum (Substack) for public recipe posts.
  • Gmail sends password-reset codes.

Deleting your data

Guest data deletes itself after 14 days (see above). With an account, go to More → Settings → Delete my account in the app. That deletes your sign-in right away. If you're the only member of your household, it also deletes all of the household's data. You can also ask us through Contact us and we'll delete it for you. Usage information tied to your account is deleted with it.

Health information

If you choose dietary needs (like diabetes-friendly or low-sodium) or allergies, they're stored with your household only to flag recipes. WFD isn't medical or nutrition advice — talk with a registered dietitian or your doctor about health concerns.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Contact

Questions or requests: use Contact us at the bottom of More in the app, the form on our support page, or email support@wfdmeals.app.
