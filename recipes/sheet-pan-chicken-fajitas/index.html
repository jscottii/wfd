Sheet-Pan Chicken Fajitas

DinnerMexican-inspiredServes 435 min

Everything roasts on one pan while you warm the tortillas, so dinner is on the table in about half an hour with one pan to wash. Leftovers make great burrito bowls the next day.

578calories
46 gprotein
56 gcarbs
18 gfat

Per serving, estimated from the ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
  • 3 bell peppers
  • 1 large onion
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 lime
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 1/2 cup sour cream

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F and line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Slice the chicken into thin strips and the peppers and onion into strips about the same size.
  3. Toss the chicken, peppers and onion with the olive oil, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika and salt right on the pan, then spread everything into one layer.
  4. Roast for 20 minutes, stirring once halfway, until the chicken is cooked through and the peppers have browned edges.
  5. Squeeze the lime over the pan and serve in warm tortillas with sour cream.

Tips

  • Crowding the pan steams the vegetables instead of browning them. Use two pans if yours is small.
  • Warm the tortillas in a dry skillet for 20 seconds a side, or wrapped in foil in the oven for the last 5 minutes.

Keeps: Fridge 3–4 days. Freeze the cooked filling (not the tortillas) up to 3 months.
