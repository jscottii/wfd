Baked Ziti with Spinach

DinnerItalian-AmericanServes 850 min

A make-ahead pasta bake that stretches to two meals. Bake one pan tonight and freeze the other for a busy night.

450calories
26 gprotein
50 gcarbs
16 gfat

Per serving, estimated from the ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ziti
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta
  • 1 egg
  • 5 oz baby spinach
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella
  • 1/2 cup grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F and grease two 8×8 pans.
  2. Boil the ziti 2 minutes short of the package time, then drain.
  3. Stir the ricotta, egg, basil and salt together in a large bowl, then fold in the spinach.
  4. Toss the drained ziti with the marinara and the ricotta mixture.
  5. Split it between the two pans and top with the mozzarella and parmesan.
  6. Cover with foil and bake 20 minutes, then uncover and bake 10 to 15 minutes more until bubbling and golden.

Tips

  • To freeze one pan, wrap it twice before baking. Bake from frozen, covered, at 375°F for about an hour, then uncovered for 15 minutes.

Keeps: Fridge 4 days. Freeze unbaked up to 3 months.
