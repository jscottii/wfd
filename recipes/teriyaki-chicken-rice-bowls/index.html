Teriyaki Chicken Rice Bowls

DinnerJapanese-inspiredServes 430 min

A homemade teriyaki sauce takes five minutes and tastes far better than the bottled kind. Pack the bowls for lunches, or serve them family style.

680calories
45 gprotein
96 gcarbs
12 gfat

Per serving, estimated from the ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 2 cups jasmine rice
  • 3 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
Advertisement

Steps

  1. Cook the jasmine rice according to the package.
  2. Whisk the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, cornstarch, garlic and ginger with 1/4 cup water.
  3. Cut the chicken thighs into bite-size pieces. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and cook the chicken until browned and cooked through, about 8 minutes.
  4. Steam the broccoli for 4 minutes until bright green and just tender.
  5. Pour the sauce over the chicken and simmer for 2 minutes until it thickens and coats the chicken.
  6. Serve the chicken and broccoli over the rice and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Tips

  • Thighs stay juicier than breasts when reheated, which makes them the better choice for meal prep.

Keeps: Fridge 4 days. Freeze the chicken and sauce up to 2 months.
