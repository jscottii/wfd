Chicken and Vegetable Fried Rice

DinnerChinese-inspiredServes 425 min

The best use for leftover rice. Cold, day-old rice fries up in separate grains instead of turning mushy.

538calories
40 gprotein
57 gcarbs
15 gfat

Per serving, estimated from the ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cooked rice
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast
  • 3 eggs
  • 2 cups frozen peas and carrots
  • 4 green onions
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp sesame oil

Steps

  1. Cut the chicken into small pieces. Heat 1 tablespoon of the vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat and cook the chicken until cooked through, about 6 minutes. Move it to a plate.
  2. Scramble the eggs in the same pan until just set, then add them to the plate with the chicken.
  3. Add the rest of the vegetable oil, the peas and carrots, the garlic and the white parts of the green onions, and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the cooked rice and press it flat. Let it sit for a minute to crisp, then stir and repeat twice.
  5. Add the chicken and eggs back with the soy sauce and sesame oil and toss until hot.
  6. Finish with the green parts of the green onions.

Tips

  • No leftover rice? Cook it, spread it on a sheet pan and chill it for 30 minutes first.

Keeps: Fridge 3 days. Reheat until steaming hot.
