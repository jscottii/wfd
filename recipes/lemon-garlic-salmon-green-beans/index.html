Lemon-Garlic Salmon with Green Beans

DinnerAmericanServes 425 min

Salmon and green beans bake side by side with a quick lemon-garlic butter. It is fast enough for a weeknight and looks like more effort than it is.

504calories
37 gprotein
10 gcarbs
34 gfat

Per serving, estimated from the ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets
  • 1 lb green beans
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 lemon
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 2 tbsp chopped parsley
Advertisement

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Trim the green beans, toss them with the olive oil and a pinch of the salt, and spread them on one side of a sheet pan.
  3. Set the salmon fillets on the other side and season with the rest of the salt and the pepper.
  4. Melt the butter with the minced garlic and the juice of half the lemon, then spoon it over the salmon.
  5. Bake 12 to 15 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily and the green beans are tender.
  6. Finish with the parsley and the rest of the lemon cut into wedges.
