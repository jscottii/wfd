One-Pot Turkey and Bean Chili

DinnerAmericanServes 645 min

A big, mild pot of chili that feeds a crowd on Sunday and becomes lunches all week. It tastes even better on day two.

381calories
29 gprotein
32 gcarbs
15 gfat

Per serving, estimated from the ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 2 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 cans (15 oz) kidney beans
  • 1 can (15 oz) black beans
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar

Steps

  1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes, then stir in the garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the ground turkey and break it up as it browns, about 6 minutes.
  3. Stir in the chili powder, cumin and oregano and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes, drained kidney beans, drained black beans, chicken broth and salt. Bring to a boil.
  5. Turn the heat to low and simmer uncovered for 25 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick.
  6. Taste for salt and serve topped with cheddar.

Tips

  • For more heat, add a chopped jalapeño with the onion.
  • Cool it quickly by spreading it in shallow containers before it goes in the fridge.

Keeps: Fridge 4 days. Freezes well for 3 months.
